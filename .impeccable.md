## Design Context

### Users
ZShop serves multilingual consumers shopping across everyday categories on mobile and desktop. Mobile users need fast discovery, legible product information, and thumb-friendly checkout; desktop users need broader comparison, persistent navigation, and efficient scanning. The primary job is to find a trustworthy product, understand price and fulfilment, and complete a purchase with confidence.

### Brand Personality
Trustworthy, restrained, curated. ZShop should feel like a dependable contemporary retailer: warm enough to be approachable, disciplined enough to make product information credible, and selective rather than noisy.

### Aesthetic Direction
A light, product-first storefront using a 2026 editorial palette: Cloud Dancer-inspired porcelain neutrals, Cool Blue for trust and navigation, Plum Noir for confident primary actions, and Persimmon reserved for price and promotion. The visual language is contemporary retail rather than luxury-nightlife: crisp photography, strong typography, flat surfaces, precise dividers, and generous whitespace. Mobile uses bottom navigation; tablet and desktop use a real responsive storefront with a persistent top navigation and wider product grids. Dark mode uses deep plum surfaces and cool blue highlights as a secondary system preference.

### Design Principles
- Product truth before promotion: never manufacture urgency or imply offers that the backend cannot verify.
- Confidence at every decision: make price, fulfilment, returns, stock, and recovery states clear near the action.
- Color has a job: Cool Blue signals navigation and trust, Plum Noir anchors primary actions, and Persimmon is reserved for commerce emphasis.
- Adapt, do not scale: mobile and desktop share information architecture while using context-appropriate navigation and density.
- Accessible by default: WCAG AA contrast, semantic controls, visible focus, zoom support, and 44px touch targets.
